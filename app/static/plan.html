A page before the map

Hello, dear adventurer.

Come in. Rest your boots for a little while. There is no test here, and there is no wrong way to begin.

How has life been treating you, lately? What has kept you company, and what has been quietly tugging at your thoughts?

Think back for a moment. What little victories have you collected along the way? What difficult adventures did you somehow make it through?

Every journey begins with a person, not a plan. You do not need to have everything figured out. You only need to know where your feet are standing now.

Tell us where you are going, where you begin, and how much time you have.
